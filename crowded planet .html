The question isn’t only how many of us there are. It’s how lightly, and how fairly, we live.

Paths toward balance

  • Educate girls and expand family planning. Where women can choose, birth rates fall, and families and ecosystems both gain.
  • Waste less food. Roughly a third of food produced is lost or thrown away, along with the land and water used to grow it.
  • Shift to clean energy. Solar, wind and efficient buildings cut emissions without cutting living standards.
  • Build compact, green cities. Dense, walkable cities with transit and parks use less land per person.
  • Protect and restore habitat. Forests, wetlands and rivers store carbon, clean water and shelter wildlife.
